Kyle proposed decision-tree troubleshooting guides to cut AHT and lift first-contact resolution; Jacquiline scoped a refund tracker that captures root cause and routes approvals; Afril targeted unnecessary escalations by requiring agents to show what they'd already checked; Noemi designed a macro refresh around our tone pillars. Several already have a counter-move: "acceptable choice over perfect choice," clearer boundaries.

That governance instinct is exactly what Month 2 is meant to develop.
